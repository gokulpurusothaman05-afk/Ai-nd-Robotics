What is the OmniBot?

Toggle

OmniBot is an autonomous bipedal humanoid robot designed for high-dexterity manipulation, spatial mapping, and dynamic indoor task execution.

What tasks can the OmniBot perform?

Toggle

From complex cleanroom sorting and tool handling to inventory logistics and collaborative household assistance.
